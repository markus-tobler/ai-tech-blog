I'm Markus Tobler. I work where business goals meet technology, helping organisations move AI from pilots to everyday use. Most of my work happens with the Microsoft stack: Power Platform, Copilot Studio, Dataverse and Azure.

## What I write about

- **AI technology**: what current models, agents and platforms can and cannot do in real organisations.
- **AI transformation**: how operating models, governance and roles change when AI becomes part of daily work.
- **Citizen development**: how business people build useful solutions with low-code and AI, safely.
- **AI enablement**: training, communities and centres of excellence that make adoption stick.

## How I write

Each article starts from something I have seen in practice. I explain the background, show the trade-offs and end with something you can decide or do. Every article states how AI helped in writing it.

## Get in touch

Spotted an error or want to suggest a topic? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/markus-tobler/ai-tech-blog/issues). You can also comment below each article.
