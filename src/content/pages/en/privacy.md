This is a private, non-commercial blog. I keep data processing to a minimum. This notice follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU GDPR.

## Responsible person

Markus Tobler, Switzerland. Contact me through an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/markus-tobler/ai-tech-blog/issues).

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the service. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Fonts

All fonts are served from this website. Your browser makes no requests to font providers.

## Visitor statistics

I use [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to count page views. GoatCounter sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses or personal identifiers. It records the page, referrer, browser, screen size and an approximate country. Read more in the [GoatCounter privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

## Comments

Comments use [giscus](https://giscus.app/), which stores them as GitHub Discussions in this blog's repository. The comment section loads only when you scroll to it. To comment, you sign in with GitHub, and GitHub's privacy terms apply. Your comments are public.

## Your rights

You can ask what data about you is processed and request its correction or deletion. Contact me as described above. Comments you wrote can be edited or deleted directly on GitHub.
