**Key takeaways**

- Most AI programmes stall on decisions, not on technology.
- This blog focuses on those decisions: what to build, who builds it and how to scale it.
- Each article ends with something you can decide or do next week.

## The gap I keep seeing

Every month brings new models, agents and platform features. Yet the conversations I have with leaders, citizen developers and architects repeat the same questions. Where do we start? Who is allowed to build what? How do we keep control without slowing everyone down?

## What you can expect

I write about four areas: AI technology, AI transformation, citizen development and AI enablement. I draw on my own project work, anonymised where needed, and I link to sources so you can check my claims.

## What to do next

Pick one AI initiative in your organisation and write down the one decision that blocks it today. Keep that decision in mind while you read the next articles.
